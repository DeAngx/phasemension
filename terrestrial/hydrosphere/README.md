# hydrosphere

Water as planetary working fluid and phase medium.

Pressure, temperature, and composition drive continuous transitions that ordinary surface intuition does not capture. Liquid, solid, supercritical, and high-pressure ice phases all exist inside Earth. Connectivity of water (and water-bearing systems) is not limited to surface rivers.

Use this space for notes and models that treat water as a distributed, addressable density medium rather than a surface resource.
