# Constraints

Hard limits and directional interests we agree to keep while working in this space.

---

## #1 — Not a UFO simulator or explorer

This is **not** a UFO / UAP simulator, detector, tracker, or exploratory vehicle.

If something in the real world later appears to resemble the mechanisms we study here, that resemblance is incidental. The work is about the underlying conceptual machinery: density shifts, sustained non-equilibrium configurations, multi-axis control of form, programmable interfaces, and the conditions under which a configuration can be held or transformed.

At most, the work may illuminate *one facet* of how certain observed phenomena could operate — conceptually. It does not aim to model, search for, or claim explanation of any specific objects, craft, or sightings.

We stay on the physics / geometry / control side. We do not drift into identification, attribution, or “what they are.”

---

## #2 — Programmable matter, not filament-in-a-box

The interest is **programmable matter** and a future model of fabrication that is different in kind from current 3D printing.

Not the familiar setup: an arm moving inside a box depositing heated plastic (or any equivalent sequential extrusion / sintering process). That is already solved enough. It is not the target.

What is being looked for is something more “room-like” — spatially distributed, field- or constraint-driven, capable of holding and transforming density and form without relying on a single mechanical effector tracing a path. Closer to matter that can be addressed and reorganized in place than to a tool that builds an object layer by layer.

The work stays oriented toward that class of possibility: multi-axis control of configuration, sustained non-default densities, programmable interfaces, and fabrication that feels more like shaping a volume than drawing a path.

---

## #3 — Terrestrial / intra-planetary systems as the primary model

The data and models we lean on are things that already exist under the surface and in the hydrosphere: water, crystals, pressure regimes, phase behavior across depth, connected volumes, and the ways Earth already moves and reorganizes matter at planetary scale.

Four classical stages of matter are not enough. The work needs a more intra-planetary system understanding — something that can speak about continuous density, geometry, and connectivity across the volume of the planet rather than treating surface and subsurface as separate domains.

One concrete orientation: what would make a tunnel from one end of the Earth to the other *conceptually coherent*? Not as engineering fantasy, but as a question about density, pressure, phase, geometry, and sustained pathways through a planetary interior. If we can reach even a partial conceptual space where that question has structure, working backward from it becomes interesting.

Earth already contains the phenomena. They are just spread across many fields. The link being sought is a way of reading those distributed facts as one system of addressable, reorganizable matter.

---

These three set the frame. Additional constraints can be added later if needed.
