# Two mechanism lines — intersection, divergence, invariance

Locked reading of the relationship between:

- **Water / continuum line** — Phases B, C, E (`e0` parcel)
- **Cube / discrete line** — Phase G (`g0` lattice)

---

## Invariant (shared spine)

These commitments are the same on both lines:

1. Observation vs intervention — pure reads; every state change is `do(...)` with logged ΔE
2. Joule ledger — \(E(t_0) + \sum \Delta E = E(t_1)\)
3. Primary tracked quantities — phase/density-like state + configurational energy (kinetic ~0 when quasi-static)
4. Representation contract — tracks relations; does not become the thing; declared scope
5. Retreat — no silent overrides, no smuggled harness, no “is the material”

**One-line invariant:**

> Any allowed change is an intervention on declared state; energy balance closes; geometry or physics may restrict *which* interventions exist; neither line may change state silently.

---

## Intersection

- Same energy model family (latent + PV stand-in on φ)
- Object as carrier of addressable state, not finished noun
- Attention on the interval where tracked state is still moving
- Failure modes: broken audit, undefined primary quantity, scope creep

---

## Divergence

| Axis | Water line | Cube line |
|------|------------|-----------|
| What authorizes change | Phase path / local `do` on one volume | Shared face + transfer amount |
| Geometry | Optional in e0 | Load-bearing in g0 |
| State motion | Local phase change | Transport between units |
| Terrestrial force | Strong (high-pressure ice) | None by design (man-made Euclidean) |
| Natural vs artificial | Reads Earth | Builds interaction machine |

They do **not** collapse. Parallel lines under one frame.

---

## What is not claimed

- Cubes are not a model of Ice VII
- Water phases are not a lattice of polygons
- Intersection does not unlock harness or room-like fabrication
- Use (F) stays gated
