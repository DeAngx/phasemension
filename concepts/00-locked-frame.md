# Locked Frame (2026-08-16)

Hard conceptual commitments for phasemension. These are not provisional preferences; they define the working stance.

---

## Stance

- Objects matter less as fixed nouns and more as carriers of properties that can be addressed and transformed.
- The static case looks ordinary. Attention belongs to the interval of controlled change.
- The work is understanding *how* and *why* reorganization of density, phase, and organization occurs — not claiming ownership or free rewrite of matter.
- Code is a way to represent and test relations. It is not a substitute for the thing. "I have a coded rock" is not a meaningful claim here.
- Retreat from overclaiming is part of the discipline. Coherence is not permission to invent what has not been forced into the open.

## Constraints (already in notes/constraints.md)

1. Not a UFO / UAP simulator or explorer. Conceptual mechanism only.
2. Programmable matter / room-like volume-addressable reorganization — not sequential filament-in-a-box fabrication.
3. Terrestrial / intra-planetary systems (water, crystals, subsurface, hydrosphere, planetary connectivity) are the primary model and data source.

## Core distinctions

**Observation vs Intervention**

- Observation: pure read of state. Energy (Joules) must be conserved. Any drift is a bug in the representation.
- Intervention: `do(X)`. Energy crosses the boundary. The ΔJoules of the intervention must be calculated and logged. Silent state overrides are forbidden.

Conservation audit (non-negotiable for any computational representation):

```
E_observed(t0) + Σ Intervention Joules (t0 → t1) = E_observed(t1)
```

**Unit**

Joules are the calculable unit for transitions and for the cost of sustaining or altering a configuration. Other accounts (geometry, topology, information) may later prove necessary; energy is the first hard ledger.

**Harness**

Continuous multi-axis re-supply of the constraints that keep a configuration from collapsing to ambient preference. Energetically: continuous, accountable intervention. A "parked" non-default density is not free.

**Density**

Treated as a driven quantity, not only a passive material property.

**Change**

The load-bearing interest is the controlled transition itself — the interval where more than one ordering remains available — not the before-state or after-state as finished objects.
